# Definition of Ready (DoR)

An issue is **Ready** for implementation only when:

- [ ] Its goal and verifiable outcome are clear.
- [ ] It has testable acceptance criteria (or an equivalent for Technical Tasks / Bugs).
- [ ] Its scope and exclusions are unambiguous.
- [ ] Dependencies and blockers are identified; no unresolved blocker prevents starting.
- [ ] Relevant system areas/modules and canonical documents are referenced.
- [ ] Critical decisions and contracts needed before implementation are agreed **or** their clarification is an explicitly scoped prerequisite task.
- [ ] The work is small enough to review and integrate safely.
- [ ] Known security risks, required permissions, and operator approvals are documented.

**Refinement may begin without DoR; implementation may not.** Completing GitHub Issue Form fields does not prove readiness. Readiness must be assessed by the person or agent leading refinement.

**Subtasks:** A subtask does not necessarily need its own full DoR or PR. For a substantial independently delegated subtask, specify at least its goal, scope, and expected result.
